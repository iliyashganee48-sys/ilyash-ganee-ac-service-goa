AC Fault Finding & Diagnosis

Common AC problems that may require inspection and proper diagnosis.

❄️ AC Not Cooling

Possible areas include dirty filters/coils, airflow, refrigerant condition, electrical operation, compressor operation or other system faults.

⚡ AC Not Powering ON

Supply, wiring, fuse/protection, PCB power section and control-related areas may need checking.

🔄 Inverter Compressor Starts Then Stops

Inverter PCB, IPM, compressor electrical condition, sensors, protection conditions and system parameters may require diagnosis.

💧 Indoor Water Leakage

Drain blockage, drain pipe condition, drain tray, installation level and indoor coil condition can be inspected.

🌀 Indoor Fan Not Running

Motor, wiring, PCB output, control signal and relevant electrical components may require checking.

🔧 Outdoor Fan Problem

Outdoor motor, electrical supply, control board and associated components can be inspected.

🔥 Compressor Not Starting

The diagnosis may include electrical supply, compressor condition, control circuit and protection-related causes.

💡 Display Not Working

Indoor PCB supply, display board, wiring and communication/control sections may need inspection.

📡 Remote Not Working

Remote batteries, remote operation, receiver section and control communication can be checked.

⚙️ PCB Short / Fault

PCB power section, protection components and other electronic components can be diagnosed according to the model.
